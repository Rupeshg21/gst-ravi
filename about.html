About SLV GST Services

A GST, income tax and accounting firm in Yadiki, Andhra Pradesh, trusted since 2016.

SLV GST Services is a GST, income tax and accounting service firm based in Yadiki, Anantapur district, Andhra Pradesh. Founded by Ravi Teja, we have served clients since 2016, online across India and in person by appointment.

What We Do

  • GST registration
  • GST return filing
  • Income tax return (ITR) filing
  • Accounting and bookkeeping

Ravi Teja, Founder

Ravi Teja leads SLV GST Services and writes the guides on this site. Each guide is dated, and rules that change each year are marked so you can confirm current limits before acting.

How We Work

  1. You call or WhatsApp us and describe what you need.
  2. We meet you by appointment, or start online if you prefer.
  3. You share documents. We prepare, file and confirm every step with you, including acknowledgement numbers.

Where We Serve

Online across India. In person by appointment in Yadiki, Tadipatri, Anantapur, Gooty and Hyderabad.
