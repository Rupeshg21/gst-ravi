Frequently Asked Questions

  • Who needs GST registration?
    Businesses above the turnover limit for goods or services, and some businesses regardless of turnover, such as certain inter-state suppliers and e-commerce sellers.
  • Is there a government fee?
    Registration on the GST portal carries no government fee. Our charge covers preparation and follow-up.
  • How long does it take?
    With clear documents it usually takes about a week.
