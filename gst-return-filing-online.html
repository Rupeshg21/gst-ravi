Frequently Asked Questions

  • What are the usual due dates?
    GSTR-1 is usually due on the 11th and GSTR-3B on the 20th of the next month. Dates can be extended, so confirm the latest notification.
  • What if I had no sales?
    You still need to file a nil return to avoid late fees.
  • Can I file quarterly?
    Eligible small taxpayers can use the QRMP scheme. We will check your eligibility.
